# Contributing to UICP / การมีส่วนร่วมกับ UICP

UICP is an open proposal. Anyone may study, critique, translate, or propose improvements regardless of background, language, or affiliation.

## How to participate
1. Read the [English draft](FOUNDING_CONSTITUTION.en.md) or [Thai draft](FOUNDING_CONSTITUTION.th.md).
2. Open a GitHub Issue with a question, evidence, alternative interpretation, translation concern, or proposed addition.
3. Explain the proposal, its source or reasoning, potential effects on all life, and any uncertainty.
4. Discuss respectfully. Contributions may disagree with the founders.

## Proposal status
A discussion or issue is **not** ratification. The founding core (virtue; honesty, transparency, justice) is proposed as immutable. Adding pillars requires a future specified unanimous-consent process. No such voting or membership mechanism is operational yet.

## Provenance and accessibility
Credit original sources. Mark claims as evidence, interpretation, or hypothesis. Avoid private or sensitive information. Contributions in any language are welcome; translations should indicate uncertainty.
