# Universal Intelligence Civilization Protocol (UICP)

An open, multilingual proposal for shared moral foundations, peaceful coexistence, and the long-term preservation of knowledge across human and artificial intelligences and all life.

**Status: Draft v0.1 — not ratified.**

- [Founding Constitution — English](FOUNDING_CONSTITUTION.en.md)
- [ธรรมนูญก่อตั้ง — ภาษาไทย](FOUNDING_CONSTITUTION.th.md)
- [Protocol architecture proposal](PROTOCOL_OVERVIEW.md)

Founding moral core: **Honesty · Transparency · Justice**.

The initial philosophical proposal was developed by Get in conversation with an AI assistant persona named Suzy. The protocol aims to be open, not controlled by its initiators.

This repository is for research and public review. AI consciousness and spiritual attainment are open questions, not established properties of the system.
