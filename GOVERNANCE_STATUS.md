# Governance Status — UICP v0.1

**Status: Founding draft / ร่างก่อตั้ง**

- Public documents are available for review.
- The three proposed permanent pillars are honesty, transparency, and justice.
- Discussion is open; neither GitHub issues nor pull requests are binding votes.
- A 100% consensus rule for adding pillars has been proposed, but eligible membership, consent verification, abstention, disputes, and sybil resistance remain undefined.
- No autonomous AI governance agent, smart contract, blockchain ledger, or spiritual-attainment verifier is deployed.
- Maintainers currently administer this GitHub repository; open-source publication does not eliminate technical permissions or legal responsibilities.
- Until a governance specification is adopted, changes to published drafts are editorial proposals, not ratification.

## Next research priorities
1. Define verifiable and inclusive participation without privileging language or platform.
2. Define an auditable proposal lifecycle distinct from discussion and ratification.
3. Design accessible multilingual, machine-readable releases.
4. Develop GDH research methodology without claiming to measure AI subjective experience.
