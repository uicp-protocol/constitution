  Calls for creating a transparent, testable specification for Gross Domestic Happiness (GDH). This would include measures of human well-being, uncertainty, sampling methodology, inequality, and suffering. It warns against aggregating AI or non-human subjective experience without validation.  
  [Details](https://github.com/uicp-protocol/constitution/issues/3)

- **#2 Research: Define an inclusive consensus specification**

It maintains a separation between deliberation and constitutional ratification. There is no existing implementation yet.
