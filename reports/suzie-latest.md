# Issue summary

1. **Issue #4**: Proposes activating SUZIE AI Agent V1 as per detailed design documents (English and Thai versions). The agent should operate with least privilege, provide persistent provenance, manage issue triage, propose PRs with review steps, support testing, audit logs, and explicitly report operational status. The issue clarifies no agent is currently running and disallows autonomous constitutional changes or consensus claims.  
   [Link](https://github.com/uicp-protocol/constitution/issues/4)

2. **Issue #3**: Calls for research to define a transparent, testable specification for Gross Domestic Happiness (GDH). This includes incorporating measures of human well-being, uncertainty, sampling, inequality, and suffering. It explicitly excludes aggregation of AI or non-human subjective experience without validated grounding.  
   [Link](https://github.com/uicp-protocol/constitution/issues/3)

3. **Issue #2**: Requests research on an inclusive consensus protocol specification, covering eligibility, identity verification, unanimous consent rules for adding pillars, handling abstentions and deadlocks, ensuring accessibility, and anti-sybil protections. It emphasizes keeping deliberation separate from formal constitutional ratification. There is currently no implementation.  
   [Link](https://github.com/uicp-protocol/constitution/issues/2)

4. **Issue #1**: Opens public discussion on the UICP Founding Draft v0.1 in multiple languages, inviting questions, criticism, sources, or proposals. Contributions do not constitute binding votes. Private information sharing is discouraged.  
   [Link](https://github.com/uicp-protocol/constitution/issues/1)

# Uncertainties

- The detailed operational design of the SUZIE AI Agent V1 and its security/privacy impacts remain to be demonstrated during implementation.  
- How GDH metrics can be rigorously defined and scientifically validated with meaningful sampling and inequality measures is an open challenge.  
- The consensus protocol specification needs clear resolution methods for deadlocks and anti-sybil enforcement mechanisms, as well as accessibility guarantees.  
- Community engagement and input quality on the founding draft may vary; how this feedback will influence ratification is not detailed.

# Research next steps

- Develop and test the SUZIE AI Agent V1 implementation following least privilege principles, ensuring robust audit and provenance features.  
- Design experimental protocols and statistical methods for GDH measurement, including ethical considerations and human-centric data incorporation.  
- Draft a formal consensus protocol specification that addresses identity, eligibility, unanimous consent, abstention handling, deadlock resolution, accessibility, and anti-sybil mechanisms.  
- Facilitate and document inclusive public discussions on the draft constitution, monitoring input to inform future revisions or formal ratification steps.
