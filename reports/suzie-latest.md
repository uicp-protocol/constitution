## 3. Research: GDH measurement specification v0.1

The specification should incorporate human well-being evidence, address uncertainty, sampling methods, inequality, and suffering measures. It warns against aggregating AI or non-human subjective experiences without validated bases.  
[Issue #3](https://github.com/uicp-protocol/constitution/issues/3)
